One of the hardest parts of my job is telling a client something they do not want to hear, especially when it goes against their own plan. That happened with a couple in the Humble, Texas area, and looking back it turned out to be one of the more important conversations I have had with a client.

## The plan they had

They were getting ready to downsize. The husband was approaching retirement, and like a lot of people in that position he had a plan already set in his mind: retire first, get used to the idea of not working, and then list the house in December once things had calmed down. It is an understandable instinct. Nobody wants to juggle a home sale on top of a major life transition.

When they brought me in to talk through the timeline, I told them what I was seeing in the Humble and greater Harris County market at that moment.

## What I told them

I told him straight out: this market has a bubble in it, and I think it is about to break. If they wanted top dollar for their home, waiting until December was the wrong move. They needed to list in the spring, while conditions in the Humble area were still working strongly in sellers' favor.

I knew that was a hard thing to hear. He had a plan, a timeline, a mental picture of how retirement was going to unfold, and I was asking him to move all of it up by months, in the middle of one of the biggest transitions of his life.

## Listing in spring

To their credit, they listened. We got the home, a four bedroom on a corner lot in Eagle Springs with a water view and no rear neighbors, on the market that spring instead of waiting for winter. The neighborhood was still seeing strong buyer demand and healthy competition for well-presented homes. We priced it from what the current market would support, marketed it to highlight what made it stand out, and found a buyer willing to pay for it.

We were under contract in nineteen days. A backup contract sat behind the first one. At the closing table in May, they sold the home for **$5,000 more than they had originally hoped to get**. That was not luck. We caught the market at the right moment instead of waiting for it to shift against us.

## What December looked like

That timing mattered more than either of us realized at the time. By December of that same year, the market had changed noticeably. The kind of quick, competitive sale they had in the spring was no longer happening for comparable homes in the area. Homes were sitting longer, buyers had more leverage, and sellers were making concessions that had not been necessary just months earlier.

When the couple saw how different things looked by the time their original December listing date would have arrived, they were relieved, and grateful, that they had trusted my read on the market instead of sticking with the original plan.

## The most valuable thing an agent brings

I share this story because it captures something that does not get talked about enough. Sometimes the most valuable thing an agent brings to the table is the market knowledge and the confidence to tell a client something that goes against their own plan, at the moment it actually matters. If I had gone along with the December timeline because it was easier, or because it was what they originally wanted, this couple would have sold into a much softer market and very likely walked away with less.

Markets in the Humble and greater Harris County area shift, sometimes faster than people expect, and the difference between a spring listing and a winter listing can be measured in real dollars. That is no reason to panic about the timing of every decision. It is a reason to have someone in your corner who is watching those shifts closely and is willing to say so.

If you are thinking about downsizing, retiring, or wondering whether now is the right time to sell in the Humble or Harris County market, do not lock in a date without first getting a read on where the market stands. Timing is a lot more than most people give it credit for. I am always glad to talk through your situation and give you an honest assessment, whatever season you are planning around.
